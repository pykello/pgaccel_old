The [sorted aggregation path](Q11-ITERATIONS.md) proves dimension
uniqueness and exact equivalence of the parent and scalar inputs. It
computes group sums and their total in one filtered scan, then applies
the resolved decimal HAVING threshold after merging stripe boundaries.

The frozen binary passed all 184 native tests, six focused ASan/UBSan
tests and all 22 SF1 answers. Q11 returns no rows at SF10 and SF100;
its nonempty SF1 result contains 1048 rows and also matches DuckDB.
[Q11.json](Q11.json) retains every trial, settings and binary hashes.

PGAccel scans resident native columns; DuckDB uses its persistent database;
ClickHouse uses MergeTree with column statistics. These are engineering
measurements on the same generated data, not audited TPC results. Q11 uses
the original SQL without a ClickHouse floating-point adapter.
