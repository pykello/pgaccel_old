The [minimum-join path](Q2-ITERATIONS.md) computes per-part minima and
projects all matching fact rows through verified dimension sequences.
Outer and child filter domains remain separate. Every minimum-cost tie
and duplicate fact survives; unsupported layouts retain general execution.

The frozen binary passed all 178 native tests and eight focused ASan/UBSan
tests. [Q2.json](Q2.json) retains every trial, settings and binary hashes.
The SF100 DuckDB margin is narrow and must be rechecked in the final
same-binary full-query sweep.

PGAccel scans resident native columns; DuckDB uses its persistent database;
ClickHouse uses MergeTree with column statistics. These are engineering
measurements on the same generated data, not audited TPC results. Q2 uses
the original SQL without a ClickHouse floating-point adapter.
